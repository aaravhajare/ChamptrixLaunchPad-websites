Frequently Asked Questions

What is abacus learning?▾

Abacus learning uses a bead frame to teach counting, place value and mental calculation. Children first manipulate the beads, then gradually move to mental visualization without the physical tool.

How does a program work?▾

Each program runs for 12 weeks with two 45‑minute classes per week. Sessions combine instruction, guided practice and home assignments.

Who can enrol?▾

Our courses are designed for children ages 5‑13. Beginners start with basic bead handling, while older learners focus on speed and competition preparation.
